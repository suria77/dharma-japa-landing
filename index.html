The practice

An old practice, and a small tool to keep count of it.

Repeating a mantra on a string of beads belongs to the Hindu and yogic traditions of India. Dharma Japa leaves the practice as it is and takes care of the counting.

Japaजप
The repetition of a mantra, spoken aloud, whispered or kept silent. Practitioners have long counted the repetitions so the mind can rest on the sound.
Malaमाला
A string of 108 beads plus one larger bead. Each bead marks one repetition, and one turn of the string is one round.
Sumeruसुमेरु
The larger bead where a round begins and ends. Tradition asks you to turn the mala around at this bead instead of crossing it. The app marks the end of the round with a closing bell.

What Dharma Japa does

  • Counts your repetitions by voice, by tap, or with a recording
  • Shows the mala you chose, one bead lit at a time
  • Plays a bell at the halfway point and at the end of each round

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Is Dharma Japa a New Age or fortune-telling product?

No. It makes no predictions, gives no readings and promises no results. It is a counter with sound and visual aids for japa, a repetition practice from the Hindu and yogic traditions.

What is japa?

Japa is the repetition of a mantra, spoken aloud, whispered or silent. Many practitioners count repetitions on a mala of 108 beads. Dharma Japa does the counting so you can stay with the sound.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or computer with a current browser. On iPhone and iPad, open it in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app. It then opens like any other app.

Does Voice Count record my voice?

No. Your browser asks for microphone permission the first time. The app measures the loudness of your voice on your own device to detect each repetition. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Manual Count needs no microphone.

What happens to the recording in Record & Repeat?

It stays in your browser while the app is open and is never uploaded. When you close the app, it is gone.

What do I receive after buying Premium?

A license code by email, together with the link to open the app. You sign in with your email and the code.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes, on up to two devices per license. You can release one device to sign in on another.

What if it is not for me?
